    Processing & review decisions

      WaterTrace / Satellite evidence. Grounded decisions.OpenCV 5 · local processing · no external fonts or maps
      YOUR STUDY AREA

      Add radar observations

      North-up GeoTIFFs, exactly two bands: VV then VH in dB. Each up to 24 MiB / 2 million pixels. Calibrate and terrain-correct upstream.

      Compare an earlier observation

      Uploads are processed locally and not retained by the server after processing. Raster parsers are not a security sandbox; use trusted files.
      METHOD / SCREEN V1

      Screen. Cross-check. Review.

      OpenCV applies a 3×3 median filter, bounded Otsu thresholds, dual-polarization agreement with a 1.5 dB margin, and connected-component filtering. Disagreement and invalid neighborhoods remain unknown.

      What this does not establish

      • No proof of crop loss, flood depth, soil waterlogging, access safety, or insurance entitlement.
      • No crop mask, terrain-shadow mask, or vegetation exclusion layer is applied. Errors can occur even in classified pixels.
      • Water may be permanent or intentionally present in rice paddies. Crop context requires human confirmation.
      • Repeated water observations do not prove continuous flooding between dates.
      • The default sectors are an illustrative survey grid, not actual farms or agricultural boundaries.

      Real data, bounded claims

      Examples use historical Sen1Floods11 training chips. Optical images are visual context only. Independent labels are used after inference in the evaluator and explicit Evidence Lab replays. Labels never drive classification or inspection queues.
